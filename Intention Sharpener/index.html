What's your broad intention?

State it plainly, for the record. Every change starts vague — this office will help you sharpen it into a concrete target.

REF. 002 — PARTICULARS

Let's get specific.

What would that actually look like in the real world? The case needs details, not impressions.

01 / THE SCOPE

Define the exact area of your life, project, or business this applies to.

02 / THE METRIC

Quantify the goal. What's the specific number or amount you're targeting?

No number yet? Try how many, how often, or how much.

03 / THE PROOF

Identify the undeniable physical evidence that this outcome has occurred.

04 / THE TIMELINE

Set a firm, specific deadline or required frequency.

05 / Now combine your answers into one specific sentence.

Your words are placed as typed, with only connecting words added. Edit the sentence below however you like.

Pressing “Build my sentence” also checks spelling. Your sentence is sent to a Google AI service that fixes misspelled words only. It never rewrites your words, and you can undo.

REF. 003 — MOTIVE

Why this intention matters

Your brain's filter only flags what's genuinely important. For the record, let's establish the stakes.

01 / THE INTERNAL DRIVER

Why does achieving this specific outcome matter to you personally?

02 / WITNESS — THE WHO

Name one specific person who benefits when you reach this goal (just their first name).

03 / WITNESS — THE IMPACT

How exactly does this outcome help them?

04 / COST IF UNRESOLVED

What's the tangible cost of not following through on this?
